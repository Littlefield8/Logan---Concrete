Concrete Foundations, Footings & Flatwork in Cache Valley

The foundation is the most important concrete on any property — everything else sits on top of it. It has to be level, square, properly reinforced, and built for Utah’s frost depth and soils. Cache County Concrete helps builders and homeowners across Cache Valley find licensed local contractors who pour footings, foundation walls and structural flatwork.

Foundation & Flatwork Services

  • Footings — poured below the frost line to prevent heaving.
  • Foundation & stem walls — formed, reinforced and poured to spec.
  • Basement & garage slabs — flat, level structural floors.
  • Shop, shed & RV pads — thicker, reinforced slabs for heavy loads.
  • Monolithic slabs — slab-on-grade for outbuildings and additions.

Why Proper Foundation Work Matters Here

  • Frost depth — footings must sit below the frost line.
  • Soil & compaction — ground evaluated and compacted.
  • Reinforcement — properly placed rebar.
  • Drainage — keep water away from footings and walls.

Foundations FAQ

How deep do footings need to be in Utah?

Footings must extend below the local frost line — typically around 30 inches in Cache Valley, built to local code.

How much does a foundation cost?

It depends on type and size. The contractor provides a free, detailed quote for your specific plans.
