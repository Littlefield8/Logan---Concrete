Concrete Work for Hyrum, Utah Homes

Homes in Hyrum, UT face the same climate challenges as the rest of Cache Valley — heavy snow, road salt, and constant freeze-and-thaw. A poorly poured slab can crack, spall or heave within a few seasons. The difference comes down to base prep, drainage, slab thickness, reinforcement, an air-entrained mix and properly placed control joints.
