Common Questions

Concrete FAQ

How much does concrete cost in Logan, UT?

Most flatwork in the Cache Valley area runs roughly $7–$15 per square foot installed, depending on the project, thickness, prep and finish. Decorative finishes like stamping cost more. Every job is different, so the licensed contractor you're referred to gives a free written quote after seeing the site.

Is it free to request a quote?

Yes — requesting a quote through this site is free and no-obligation. Call or text (435) 495-4170 or use the form below, and a licensed local concrete contractor can follow up, usually within a day or two.

What's the best time of year to pour concrete in Utah?

Late spring through early fall is ideal, while temperatures stay above freezing during curing. Concrete can be poured in cooler weather with blankets and mix adjustments, but pouring into a hard freeze is generally avoided.

How long before I can use new concrete?

You can usually walk on it after about 24 hours. For a driveway, wait roughly 7 days before driving on it and about 28 days before parking heavy vehicles, so it reaches full strength.

Is concrete better than asphalt or pavers?

For most uses here, concrete lasts longer, needs less maintenance and adds more value than asphalt, and unlike pavers it's one solid slab with no joints for weeds to grow through. A local contractor can help you weigh the options for your project.
